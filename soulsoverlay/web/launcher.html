!

This game's preset is a work in progress and hasn't been tested in game yet. The positions of the boss health bar and "YOU DIED" are estimates, so deaths and PBs may not be counted correctly.

For the best results, click Calibrate after switching and mark where the game draws them.

Settings
SoulsOverlay is still running

It keeps counting from the tray, by the clock in the corner of the taskbar. Click its icon there to open this window again, or right-click it and choose Quit to stop it.

Rather have it quit when you close the window? Turn off Keep running in the tray in Settings.

Open the Overlay Editor?

It opens in your web browser, where you can change the overlay's colours, text and the NEW PB effect, and see them on a live preview of the boss bar.

Nothing changes on stream until you click Save there. Then OBS picks it up straight away. Undo changes and Reset all take you back if you don't like it.

Hotkeys

They work while you play, even with the game in front. Untick any you don't want, so you can't press them by accident. To change one, click its keys and press the new ones.
